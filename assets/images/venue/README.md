## Night venue illustration

`gbk-concert-map-night-v3.png` is original AI-generated artwork created with imagegen
on 11 October 2026, inspired by the user's night-stadium concept. It is not a photo
or an official GBK plan. The image contains no event names, labels or prices.

`js/ticket-zones.js` supplies HTML controls over the artwork; `css/venue-night.css`
sets their percentage coordinates against its 3:2 aspect ratio. Public venue prices
are read from Supabase per show. Do not bake prices or official entry gates into it.
The previous `gbk-concert-map-v2.png` remains available as the older illustration.
